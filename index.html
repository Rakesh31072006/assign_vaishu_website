💌 A Letter From My Heart

My Dearest Vaishnavi — My Vaishuu ❤️

2 November 2003 — the day the world became a little more beautiful because you were born. 🌸✨

Happy Birthday to the most beautiful person in my world. ❤️

Today isn't just another birthday. It's a reminder of how lucky I am to have you in my life, Vaishuu.

Every smile, every conversation, every little moment with you has become a beautiful memory that I never want to lose. Even though we are in a long-distance relationship, you still have a special place in my heart that nobody else could ever take. 🌙❤️

Distance may keep us apart physically, but it can never change what you mean to me. Every mile between us only reminds me how precious our moments together really are.

You are more than just someone I love. You are a part of my happiness, my memories, my dreams, and my future. 🌙❤️

🤞 My Promise To You

Vaishuu, I promise that no matter how far apart we are, I will never let the distance make you feel alone. I promise to keep choosing you, supporting you, understanding you, and protecting the little happiness we have together.

I can't promise that every day will be perfect, but I can promise that I'll always try to make our story worth remembering. And whenever life gets difficult, I'll remember why I chose you in the first place. ❤️

So today, Vaishuu, I don't just wish you a happy birthday.

I wish for more mornings where I get to wake up knowing you're mine, more nights where I can hold you close, more silly conversations, more memories, more laughter, and one day, no more distance between us. 🥹❤️

Until that day comes, keep a little piece of my heart with you, because you already have all of mine. ❤️

And if I had to choose you again in every lifetime,
I would still choose you. Every single time. ♾️❤️

Happy Birthday, my beautiful Vaishnavi. 🎂🌸

2 November will always be special to me — because it gave me you. ❤️

You are not just a memory in my story...
You are the story I want to keep writing forever. ✨❤️
